# 1. Course Overview

## Course Positioning

This is a **practical, job-oriented course for Sales Operations, Sales Intelligence, CRM Governance and Revenue Operations roles**.

Students will not simply learn how to use ChatGPT or make Power BI charts.

They will learn how to:

**Raw Sales Data → Clean Data → CRM → Funnel → Dashboard → Insight → AI → Automation → Action**

The course is based around the responsibilities found in real Sales Intelligence / CRM Governance roles, including dashboarding, lead lifecycle management, CRM hygiene, AI enablement, market intelligence, SOP creation and cross-functional coordination.

---
