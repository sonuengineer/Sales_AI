# How to Build: Sales Intelligence, CRM & AI Automation Mastery

## 1. What MasterPrompt.md Is About

`MasterPrompt.md` is a blueprint for a career-focused training program called **Sales Intelligence, CRM & AI Automation Mastery**.

It is designed to teach learners how a modern B2B sales operation works end to end:

```text
Raw sales data → cleaned data → CRM process → funnel analysis → dashboard
→ insight → AI support → automation → sales action
```

The program is **not** meant to be a collection of disconnected tool tutorials (for example, “learn Excel,” “learn ChatGPT,” or “learn Power BI”). Its core promise is that a student can build and explain a working sales-operations system that a company could use.

It combines five practical areas:

1. **Sales analytics** — KPI tracking, funnel conversion, pipeline, TAT, stale leads and performance analysis.
2. **CRM operations and governance** — data model, field rules, workflows, validation, ownership and data quality.
3. **Lead management** — scoring, qualification, routing, follow-up and lifecycle control.
4. **AI and automation** — research, qualification suggestions, summaries, follow-up drafts and no-code workflows.
5. **Business operations** — SOPs, requirements, stakeholder coordination and competitive intelligence.

The recommended course length is 72–74 hours over roughly 10–12 weeks, with 10 modules, practical labs, mini-projects and one final capstone.

## 2. What You Can Achieve With It

### For students

If delivered well, students can leave with a portfolio that demonstrates job-ready capability for roles such as:

- Sales Intelligence Analyst
- Sales Operations Analyst
- CRM Analyst or CRM Governance Specialist
- Revenue Operations Analyst
- Sales/Business Analyst
- Marketing Operations Analyst

Their portfolio can include:

1. Excel sales-analysis workbook
2. Power BI Sales Intelligence dashboard
3. CRM data model and field dictionary
4. Lead-scoring model
5. Lead assignment and stale-lead workflow
6. AI lead qualification prompt/workflow
7. AI follow-up assistant workflow
8. Competitive battle cards and market-research repository
9. SOP library
10. Management recommendation presentation

### For your education business

This can become more than a course. The natural business ladder is:

```text
Student course
  → career cohort / mentorship
  → corporate team training
  → CRM process consulting
  → dashboard, automation and AI implementation services
```

Its strongest market position is:

> Build the sales operations system companies actually need.

That differentiates it from low-cost, tool-only courses. The outcome is a portfolio and a practical business system, not merely a completion certificate.

## 3. Recommended Scope for Version 1

Do not build the full 72-hour version first. Launch a validated **Version 1 cohort** with one coherent tool stack:

| Area | Version 1 choice | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spreadsheet | Excel or Google Sheets | Core analytics and accessible datasets |
| Dashboard | Power BI | Strong portfolio value for sales-analytics roles |
| CRM | HubSpot Free | Easier hands-on setup for beginners |
| AI | ChatGPT | Use one primary tool and teach transferable prompting |
| Automation | Make.com or Zapier | Choose one; Make is often better for visual workflow teaching |
| Knowledge base | Notion or Google Drive | Store battle cards, SOPs and templates |

Teach Salesforce, Zoho and Dynamics as conceptual comparisons only. Trying to provide deep training in all CRMs will make the course shallow and difficult to operate.

## 4. Build in the Right Order

Build backward from the final student outcome.

```text
Capstone system
  → datasets and expected answers
  → templates and rubrics
  → labs and mini-projects
  → lesson plans and slides
  → recorded videos
  → landing page and cohort launch
```

This order matters: it ensures that every lesson gives students a capability required for the capstone.

## 5. Step-by-Step Build Plan

### Phase 1 — Define the learner and outcome (Days 1–3)

Decide these items before creating content:

- Primary learner: choose one first, for example fresh BBA/MBA graduates or working sales-operations professionals.
- Delivery: live cohort, recorded program, or hybrid.
- Primary outcome: portfolio for employment, performance improvement at work, or both.
- Tool access: list all free trials/accounts needed and provide setup instructions.
- Capstone business: select one fictional B2B company and keep it consistent throughout the course.

Write a one-page course charter containing the target learner, starting skill level, final portfolio, time commitment, tools and assessment standard.

### Phase 2 — Build the capstone first (Weeks 1–2)

Create one realistic but fictional B2B scenario, such as a SaaS company selling HR software to mid-market businesses. Produce a deliberately messy dataset of roughly 5,000–10,000 leads.

Create these source files:

- `leads_raw.xlsx` — IDs, sources, industries, company size, owner, dates, status and value
- `activities_raw.xlsx` — calls, emails, meetings and last activity dates
- `campaigns_raw.xlsx` — campaign spend, leads and campaign metadata
- `opportunities_raw.xlsx` — stage, proposal, deal value and close dates
- `competitors.md` — five competitors with realistic positioning notes
- `business-brief.pdf` or `.md` — company goals, target segments, sales-team structure and pain points

Ensure the data contains intentional problems: duplicates, blank owners, inconsistent status labels, invalid dates, missing values and stale records. These create the learning exercises.

Then build the complete solution yourself:

1. Clean the data.
2. Design CRM fields and governance rules.
3. Define lead score and assignment logic.
4. Build the Power BI dashboard.
5. Create two AI workflows: qualification and stale-lead follow-up.
6. Create competitor battle cards.
7. Write the SOP library.
8. Create the management presentation.

If a deliverable is unclear to you, students will also find it unclear; fix it before recording lessons.

### Phase 3 — Create reusable teaching assets (Weeks 2–3)

For every capstone deliverable, prepare these assets:

| Asset | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Starter file | Gives students the correct starting point |
| Completed solution | Provides instructor reference and post-submission feedback |
| Lab brief | States scenario, task, rules and expected output |
| Step-by-step demo | Supports beginners during guided practice |
| Rubric | Makes evaluation consistent |
| Cheat sheet | Helps students apply concepts independently |

Essential templates to create first:

- CRM field-mapping template
- CRM data-quality checklist
- lead-scoring worksheet
- lead-routing rules template
- Power BI KPI definition sheet
- dashboard-review checklist
- prompt-library template
- SOP template
- BRD/requirement template
- issue tracker and RACI matrix
- capstone assessment rubric

### Phase 4 — Convert the curriculum into lessons (Weeks 3–4)

Keep each normal lesson to 8–15 minutes. Practical build-along demos may run 15–25 minutes.

For every module, define:

```text
Business problem → concepts → real example → tool demo → guided build
→ practice task → quiz → capstone connection
```

Recommended minimum lesson plan:

| Module | Main practical output |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Sales Operations Fundamentals | Funnel KPI tracker |
| 2. Excel for Sales Intelligence | Excel funnel dashboard |
| 3. Power BI | Sales Intelligence Command Center |
| 4. CRM Governance | CRM data model and workflow |
| 5. Lead Management | Scoring, routing and TAT system |
| 6. Generative AI | Research, qualification and reporting prompts |
| 7. Automation | AI lead qualification workflow |
| 8. Market Intelligence | Competitive repository and battle cards |
| 9. SOPs and Requirements | SOP, BRD and issue tracker |
| 10. Capstone | Complete sales intelligence system |

### Phase 5 — Pilot before fully recording (Weeks 5–8)

Run a small live beta cohort of 10–20 learners. Teach live or hybrid before making polished recordings.

Measure:

- Attendance and completion rate
- Time required for each lab
- Quiz scores
- Where students become blocked
- Tool-account/setup problems
- Capstone completion rate
- Student portfolio quality
- Testimonials and permission to show anonymized student work

Update content from actual learner friction. Only then record the refined version.

### Phase 6 — Package and launch (Weeks 8–10)

Prepare these launch assets:

- Landing page with outcome-led messaging
- Curriculum page and tool requirements
- Sample dashboard screenshots/video
- Capstone case-study page
- Instructor profile and credibility proof
- Student onboarding checklist
- Payment/refund/attendance policies
- Email/WhatsApp nurture sequence
- Application form, if positioning it as a premium cohort

Lead with the capstone and portfolio. Avoid leading with total hours or a list of software names.

## 6. Assessment and Completion Standard

Use practical evidence rather than only multiple-choice quizzes.

| Component | Recommended weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Module quizzes | 10% |
| Labs and mini-projects | 35% |
| Documentation and SOPs | 15% |
| Capstone system | 30% |
| Final presentation | 10% |

Suggested certificate rule: at least 80% content completion, 70% overall assessment score and successful capstone submission.

## 7. Quality and Safety Rules

- Use fictional, anonymized or properly authorized data only.
- Never put customer personal data, confidential CRM exports or API keys into public AI tools.
- Teach students to verify AI-generated research, claims and recommendations.
- Make human review mandatory before automated messages are sent to leads.
- Define every KPI precisely; for example, document what counts as an MQL, SQL, opportunity and TAT start/end time.
- Explain that a dashboard can reveal a problem but cannot establish cause without further investigation.

## 8. Immediate Next Actions

Start with these five deliverables this week:

1. Write the one-page course charter.
2. Select the Version 1 tool stack: Excel, Power BI, HubSpot, ChatGPT, Make and Notion are a strong default.
3. Write the fictional B2B capstone brief.
4. Create the raw CRM dataset and a completed instructor solution.
5. Create the capstone rubric plus the first three templates: KPI definitions, CRM field mapping and lead scoring.

Once these exist, the course has a real foundation. Every video, lab and marketing claim can then be built around a demonstrable student outcome.

## 10. Key Decision

The most important decision is to treat this as an **outcome-based professional program**, not an information-based course. The capstone should be the product: lessons, templates, assessments and marketing all exist to help a student build it.
